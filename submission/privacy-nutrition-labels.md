# Privacy Nutrition Labels

**Data Linked to You:**
- **Contact Info:** Email address (Used for account authentication and app functionality).
- **Identifiers:** User ID (Firebase UID used to link your data to your account).
- **User Content:** Prompts and ratings (Used to store comparison history and compute aggregate rankings).

**Data Not Linked to You:**
- None.

**Data Used for Tracking:**
- None.

**Purposes for Data Collection:**
- **App Functionality:** All data collected is strictly for providing the core features of Spec: account authentication, app functionality, and infrastructure.
