**Description:**
Spec is where you decide which AI model to trust for your work. Stop wondering whether you should use GPT-4, Claude, or Gemini for a specific task. Type your prompt once, and all three models stream their answers in parallel. 

It is built for the operator. If you spend your day switching between model tabs, Spec brings them together. Rate the answers you receive, and over time, your history becomes a personalized map of which model handles code best, which writes best, and which thinks best.

Your ratings join the community's to build the first honest ranking of AI models, determined by the people actively using them. You can search your past comparisons anytime to find that one perfect answer from weeks ago.

Spec gives you 5 free comparisons per day. If you already pay for model access, simply enter your own API keys in Settings to use Spec entirely unlimited.
